About

To be the best mentor to my students and contribute towards the overall development of my institution, while constantly updating my skills to make a mark in the field of research and education.

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical & Electronics Engineering at Godavari Global University (erstwhile Godavari Institute of Engineering and Technology, Autonomous), and an AICTE Industry Fellow at Garuda Aerospace Limited, where I work on tethered drone R&D and train Remote Pilots on small and medium class drones. My research sits at the intersection of power electronic converter design, solar PV and EV grid integration, and AI/ML‑based control for microgrids and power quality — an intersection reflected in a filed patent portfolio of 60 inventions (4 granted across India and the UK), 50+ peer‑reviewed publications, and six authored textbooks. I received the Research Excellence Award at AMMEL 2026 (University of Malaya, Malaysia) and the Faculty Excellence Award for Research and Publications from SNS Institutions in 2024.

Research focus

Six threads that recur across my patents, journal papers, and conference work.

01 · CONVERTERS

Power electronic converters

Self‑lift Luo, SEPIC, Landsman, Cuk, Zeta, KY and Z‑source converter topologies for renewable and EV interfacing.

02 · PV/GRID

Solar PV & grid integration

MPPT algorithms, multilevel and quasi‑Z‑source inverters, and power quality improvement for grid‑tied PV.

03 · EV

EV charging infrastructure

On‑board and off‑board chargers, wireless and fast‑charging schemes, PV‑fed charging stations.

04 · AI/ML

AI for energy systems

ANN, ANFIS, fuzzy‑logic and RNN‑based control; PSO/GWO/firefly optimization for MPPT and fault diagnosis.

05 · MICROGRIDS

Microgrids & power quality

DFIG wind systems, D‑STATCOM, DVR, UPQC, droop control and hybrid AC/DC microgrid design.

06 · UAV

UAV & remote sensing

Tethered drone R&D at Garuda Aerospace; embedded data‑acquisition systems for unmanned aerial vehicles.
